Vision and Mission of the MIT ADT University  
 
Vision:  
 -
MIT ADT University aspires to be the University of Eminence by amalgamating Art, 
Design and Technology. The University aim to have a transformative impact on society 
through holistic education, multidisciplinary research ethos, innovation and 
entrepreneurial culture.  
 
Mission:  
 
The Mission of MIT -ADT University is to provide impetus to faculty, learners, and staﬀ 
by developing their innate intellectual capabilities, creative abilities, and entrepreneurial 
mind -set for the socio-economic development of the Nation.  
We empower learners to become adaptive and agile global professionals through unique 
specialized programs building academia-industrial partnership.  
We nurture learners to be intellectually curious, technologically equipped, mentally 
sound, physically ﬁt, spiritually inclined, socio -culturally sensitive, environmentally 
conscious through continuous holistic education for the ever-evolving world.  
We Provide technology-enabled learner-driven curriculum, value added courses, 
simulated learning environments, state of the art infrastructure, and opportunities for 
community engagement.

1. ABOUT THE UNIVERSITY 
1.1 Genesis and Evolution 
Rev. Prof. Dr. Vishwanath D. Karad, a visionary figurehead, formed a group of institutions. 
It was established with the goals of delivering top -notch education and sparking a social 
revolution. The MAEER group, which was founded to support Maharashtra's hi gher 
education institutions and provide students additional platforms to fulfil global demands, 
has a similar attitude to MIT ADT University. Early in the 1980s, committed academics and 
social thinkers established the Maharashtra Academy of Engineering Edu cational and 
Research (MAEER) trust. On May 1, 1983, MAEER presented the first private engineering 
college, known as MIT, to the city of Pune. The MIT Group has built a reputation as the most 
reputable brand of higher education in engineering, science and technology, medicine, and a 
variety of other subjects from its modest beginnings of teaching a small number of students 
in two classrooms in 1983. With more than ten campuses in Maharashtra spread across 
thousands of acres of land, more than 70,000 student s, and more than 84 institutions by 
2015, the MIT Group had expanded into a sea of higher education. The institution has been 
expanding rapidly.  
1.2 Profile of the University 
The MIT Educational Group created history in 2016 when it opened the doors of its first 
institution on the breathtaking Vishwaraj Baug campus in Loni Kalbhor. The business 
acquired property that belonged to the late Indian actor Shri Raj Kapoorji in 2001. His 
tireless work enabled Rev. Prof. Dr. Vishwanath Karad to realize his goal of creating a 
university with an innovative and high-calibre culture that was strongly rooted in the ideals 
of peace and development. Accordingly, MIT -ADT University has been est ablished in the 
year 2016 by the Government of Maharashtra vide MIT Art, Design and Technology 
University Act, 2015 (Mah. Act. No. XXXIX of 2015) and the university is in operation from 
academic year 2016-17.   
The University Grant Commission (UGC), New Delhi has recognised the MIT Art, Design and 
Technology University, Pune vide its letter No. F -8-33/2015 (CPP -1/PU), dated 10th 
November 2016 and University is empowered to issue the degrees specified in Section 2 2 of 
University Grants Commission Act, 1956. 

--- Page 5 ---
 
 
  
The MIT ADT University is approved by Maharashtra state Government, the Gazette 
published on the 21st of August 2015. 
The MIT ADT University is situated at Loni Kalbhor, Pune, and it is spread over 85 acres of 
land on the banks of Mula-  Mutha river. It is a leash green campus with state- of-the-art 
facilities. 
The university, which has 20 constituent Schools, offers intensive undergraduate, 
postgraduate, and doctoral programmes in Engineering, Fine Arts, Design, Management, 
Marine Engineering, Bioscience Engineering, Food Technology, Film and Theatre, Drama, 
Music, Broadcasting and Journali sm, Humanities, Holistic Development, Education and 
more. The University now has more than 1 5000 students enrolled, and that number is 
growing regularly. MIT ADT University is a perfect example of how entrepreneurship, 
innovation, and creativity can coexist. Faculty members at the university are always engaged 
in research and instruction to widen students' knowledge and ethical perspectives. 
Students have an amazing chance to investigate the most significant issues of the twenty-first 
century while simultaneously learning about modern literature, art, technology, design, etc. 
The MIT ADT University features creative and technological clusters under which all the 
schools collaborate to enhance the convergence of art, design, and technology. One of the 
university's main advantages is its technology cluster. The educational institutions in this 
cluster offer a mix of conventional and alternative courses.  The creative cluster, on the other 
hand, is a remarkable cluster that unites numerous arts forms and encompasses the faculties 
of art, fine art, and applied art. Music, Film, and Theatre are just a few of the creative fields 
from which the univers ity has carefully chosen courses. A multidisciplinary education and 
the opening of doors to knowledge, innovation, and creativity are the university's one and 
only objectives for its students. The University strives to develop students into physically 
healthy, cognitively strong, emotionally intelligent, spiritually elevated, and holistically 
developed persons via many courses it offers. The University has reiterated its commitment 
to helping students and alumni utilize their education to improve themselves and society, as 
well as to learn to live in peace with one another. Due to its excellent facilities and state- of-
the-art curriculum provided in its graduate and postgraduate schools, MIT ADT University 
continues to set the highest standards for creating and disseminating knowledge in India and 
around the globe.

The "Best Campus Award" was given to MIT ADT University, a multifunctional institution

sustainability and a green atmosphere.

access to necessary stationery needs.

quick transportation for students.

safe financial transactions for students and staff. 
viii. Grooming and Saloon Facilities: Inside University campus, grooming facilities for girls 
and salon services for boys are provided, ensuring convenient access to essential grooming 
amenities for all students.

4.1. Allocation of Seats Reserved for National and State CET Examinations          
for Professional Degree Programmes. 
MIT Art, Design, and Technology University is committed to providing high -quality 
education while ensuring a transparent and fair admission process for professional degree 
programs. As per the Maharashtra Private Universities (Establishment & Regulation) A ct, 
2023, under section 41 (4), forty percent of the total approved intake capacity is to be 
reserved for students domiciled in the state of Maharashtra. In line with our commitment to 
admitting meritorious students at both national and state levels, the allocation of the 
reserved seats for Common Entrance Test (CET) candidates as follows: 
▪ 60% Quota of the total seats will be allocated to other candidates through the 
National CET’s (such as JEE/NEET/NATA/DAT/CAT/MAT/ATMA/GATE etc.), 
enabling students from across the country to participate and ensuring a competitive 
and diverse student base. 
▪ 40 %  Quota of the total seats will be allocated to Maharashtra state domiciled 
candidates through the State CET’s (such as MHT-CET/PERA etc.), giving local 
students an opportunity to access professional education while maintaining state-
level representation. 
▪ If the candidates are not available for either National or State CETs, then seats will 
be transferrable to the available CET’s candidate to ensure no seat remains vacant. 
  This allocation seeks to balance national and state-level talent, ensuring that students 
with exceptional merit from all regions have the opportunity to enrol, while also safeguarding 
access for local students. The implementation of this structure will con tribute to upholding 
a high academic standard, fostering diversity, and enhancing the university’s standing within 
the professional education sector.

5. A DMISSION GUIDELINES FOR EWS/NRI/OCI/FN/CIWGC
5.1  Admissions under Economically Weaker Sections (EWS): 
As per the provisions made by the Government of Maharashtra (Resolution No. -4019/31/16-
, dated 12th February 2019) 10% seats shall be reserved for Economically Weaker Section 
(EWS) candidates. These 10% seats shall be over & above the sanctioned intake for the 
respective course. 
 As per the provision of Government of Maharashtra GR Misc -2022/ pk.91/vishi -4, dt 
22.06.2023, as per the merit 50% Concession in Programme fees/ Scholarship will be given 
to the 10% of admitted students, belonging to Economically Weaker Section in the respective 
programme/course. 
 The Institute shall ensure that 10% of reservation for Economically Weaker Section (EWS) 
as per the reservation policy for admission, without affecting the reservation percentage of 
SC/ST/OBC/General. 
NOTE: EWS vacant seats shall be considered for the lateral entry to Second Year Course(s) 
with the same reservation policy for admission. 
5.2 Admissions will be based on marks or grades obtained in the qualifying examinations as 
per eligibility criteria, and availability of seat(s). The admissions process is based on a first -
come, first-served basis. 
Under-Graduate (UG) Programmes: The candidate must have passed the Std. 
10+2 or its equivalent examinations from the recognized institution approved by the 
Government.  
Post-Graduate (PG) Programmes: The candidate must have a bachelor’s degree 
or its equivalent Degree from any recognized University approved by Government (AIU).  
5.3 MIT-ADT University follows UGC / AICTE - specified eligibility norms while reviewing 
international students’ admission applications. To take admission for any programme at 
MIT-ADT university under international category, the admission categories of international 
candidates are as mentioned below:  
A. Foreign Nationals Students: A student is eligible to apply as a Foreign National
(FN) if he/she is a citizen of any country other than India and holds a foreign passport (FN - 
SAARC countries: Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka 
and FN-Non-SAARC Countries).  

--- Page 22 ---
 
 
  
B. CIWGC/CIWSEA Students: Children of Indians working in Gulf Countries & 
Southeast Asian countries, (the candidates whose parents are working at Gulf countries or 
Southeast Asian countries) only are eligible under this category.  
C. OCI Students: A student is eligible to apply as OCI if he/she has been granted 
Overseas Citizenship of India and has an OCI card. 
D. NRI: A student is eligible for NRI Category if he/she is a Non -Resident Indian 
(Other than Gulf & Southeast Asia Countries).  
Definition of Non -Resident Indian (NRI) for Admission Eligibility:  A 
person who is 'not ordinary resident' as defined under sub -section (6) of section 6 of the 
Income Tax Act 1961 and has been issued a Non -Resident Indian certificate accordingly by 
an Indian Mission or Post abroad and includes his child or ward as defined in the Gaurdians 
and Wards Act, 1890. 
5.4    Introduction/ Continuation of Seats for Non-Resident Indian(s): 
Five percent (5%) of seats within the “Approved Intake” shall be allowed for admission under 
NRI category. 
Competent Authority for admission shall prepare a merit list of Applicants by inviting 
applications from eligible NRI students and admit strictly on merit basis. 
 In the event of non -availability of students in NRI seats, the seats shall be given to general 
candidates as per merit. 
5.5 Introduction/Continuation of Supernumerary Seats for Foreign 
Nationals/Overseas Citizen of India (OCI)/Children of Indian Workers in Gulf 
Countries: 
Fifteen percent (15%) supernumerary seats over and above the “Approved Intake” per Course 
shall be approved for AICTE approved Institutions and University Departments, for 
admitting students from Foreign Nationals/ Overseas Citizen of India/ Children of In dian 
Workers in the Gulf Countries. One third (1/3) of these 15% seats shall be reserved for the 
Children of Indian Workers in the Gulf Countries.

8. RESERVATIONS IN ADMISSIONS
Reservation Policy as prescribed by the Government of Maharashtra from time to time 
will be applicable for the admissions. 
8.1      Territorial Reservation: 
As per section 41 (4) of the Maharashtra Private Universities (Establishment and 
Regulation) Act, 2023, “Out of total approved intake capacity, forty percent of the 
seats shall be reserved for the students domiciled in the State of Maharashtra. 
Note: Forty percent of seats are reserved for Maharashtra. That means sixty percent 
of seats are open. Those seats can be allotted to anyone including Maharashtra 
Candidates, as these seats are not reserved for OMS. 
8.2     Social or Vertical Reservation: 
The Reservations given below shall be applicable only to the Domiciled candidates of 
the State of Maharashtra. Candidates domicile of other than Maharashtra State, even 
if belonging to a reserved category as per the reservation policy of their respective

10. PAYMENT OF FEES AND REFUND RULES
Academic Year 2026-27 
A) Applicable for All Schools UG / PG / Integrated / Diploma /
Cer
tificate Programmes (Except Centre of Distance and Online
Education {CDOE})
1. APPLICABILITY AND ENFORCEMENT:
i) It shall be applicable to the candidates who are taking the admission for
Undergraduate, Postgraduate, Research scholars, Diploma and Certificate
programmes in the A.Y. 2026-2027 offered by MIT ADT University.
ii) It shall come into force with immediate effect and shall have regulatory force
on extant as well as future grievances over issues and matters covered herein.
2. PAYMENT OF FEES:
As per O.118 of the Academic Ordinances, 2021 of MIT ADT University, The
University follows a specialized credit -based semester system, therefore registration at the 
beginning of each semester on the prescribed dates announced in the Academic Calendar, is 
mandatory for every student till she/he completes her/his programme. The semester 
registration by the student at the beginning of each semester is mandatory with 
the payment of applicable fees. Without registration, any academic activity (course / 
seminar / practical / term project /etc.) undergone by a student will not be counted towards 
the requirements of her/his degree.  
3 LATE FEES PAYMENT OF FEES: 
If any student pays the fees after the due date, then, 
a. If paid within 30 days after the due date, a late fee of ₹ 50/- per day will
       be charged. 
b. If paid after 30th day but before the 60th day after the due date, the late
       fee of ₹ 50/- per day for the first 30 days and ₹ 100/- per day after the 
      30th day will be charged. 
c. If the fee is not paid even after the 60th day of the due date, then the
       name of the student shall be struck off from the records of the Institute 
       and University with prior permissions of the authorities. 
d. In case, the name of the student is struck off from the records of the
Institute and University, then Transfer Certificates or recommendations
for admission to the other Institutes/colleges will not be issued to the
student or parent.

13.  Scholarship/ Concession for Economically Weaker Sections 
13.1 General:    
Concession in fee for the Student from Economically Weaker Section (EWS): The 
Government of Maharashtra has issued GR No. Misc -2022/ pk.91/ vishi -4, dt. 
22.06.2023, providing 50% (fifty percent) concession / scholarship in total fees to 10% 
(ten percent) students as per merit, belonging to the Economically Weaker Section, as 
per Govt norms, without considering cast or category, admitted in self -financed 
universities. This concession is to be given for completing the program. Students 
admitted in 2023-24 onward will be entitled for the concession/Scholarship. 
The students from Maharashtra, whose total family income is less than Rs. 
8,00,000/- (Rs. Eight Lakhs), per year are eligible to get the EWS Concession / 
Scholarship 
13.2 Procedure for merit list preparation:  
The process of giving Concession/Scholarship is to be implemented at the time of 
admission and a report of the same, including the details of the students to whom 
Concession is given, is to be submitted to the Government of Maharashtra within one 
month from the last date of admission. 
13.2.1 Distribution of seats for various entrance exams:  
Accordingly, it is communicated to all the Deans and Directors that the same be 
brought to the notice of Students and Parents coming for Admission and applications 
be accepted from the eligible students at the time of admission in the prescribed 
format, wi th specific information to the student that he will be eligible to get the 
Concession if he satisfies the requirements of Merit and EWS. 
After Admissions are completed, in case of Schools where admissions are given based 
on JEE and MH-CET, program-wise two separate Merit Lists of the admitted students 
be prepared based on the students who appeared for JEE and MH-CET. In the case of 
schools, where admissions are given based on different CETs, different merit Lists as 
per their CETs be prepared. For any programs where admissions are given without 
CET, a merit list be prepared on marks obtained at 12th standard. 
Statement of the program wise list specifying the qualifying examination (MH -CET, 
JEE, NEET, NATA, 12th std., Diploma, Graduation) is attached herewith as 
Annexure-A.  All School will prepare the Merit Lists accordingly.   
While calculating the 10% eligible students for Concession / Scholarship, 50% be 
taken from JEE list and 50% from the MH-CET list, where admissions are given based 
on JEE and MH -CET and in case of other Schools Lists as per their CETs. Then, 
documents be called from the students, who have not submitted at the time of 
admission, giving four days’ time to submit the documents to prove their entitlement 
for EWS.  A Committee be constituted in the School under the Chairmanship of the 
Dean/Director for scrutiny of documents.  Based on the report of the Committee, lists 
of students eligible for EWS Concession/ Scholarship (50% from JEE List and 50% 
from MHT-CET List) be prepared and submitted to the Registrar’s office.

14. Education Loan Facility and Other State Schemes 
14.1 PM Vidyalaxmi –  
The PM Vidyalaxmi Scheme is a Government of India initiative that provides a single-window 
online platform for students to apply for education loans from multiple banks. 
Students admitted to MIT Art, Design and Technology University, Pune may avail of 
education loans under this scheme for pursuing higher education. 
The scheme covers loans for tuition fees and other academic expenses as per bank norms. 
Loan sanction, amount, interest rate, and repayment terms are decided by the respective 
lending bank. 
Students can apply online through the Vidyalaxmi Portal: https://pmvidyalaxmi.co.in 
14.2 MNSSBY – Bihar Student Credit Card Scheme  (For Bihar State Domiciled 
Students) - 
Students who are domiciled in the State of Bihar may avail financial assistance under the 
Bihar Student Credit Card Scheme, implemented by the Government of Bihar under the 
Mukhyamantri Nishchay Swayam Sahayata Bhatta Yojana (MNSSBY). 
14.3 E – Kalyan Scholarship (For Jharkhand State Domiciled Students) - 
The Government of Jharkhand operates the E- Kalyan Scholarship to increase participation 
of Scheduled Tribe, Scheduled Caste, and Backward Class students in post-matric education, 
to provide them with equal educational opportunities, encourage them, and reduce dropout 
rates. 
Students can apply for the Post-Matric Scholarship through the link: e-Kalyan.cgg.gov.in.
